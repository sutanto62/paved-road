      where the numbers come from

      • Guardrail decision, under 100 ms: a Kyverno test with 17 pod-security policies, published by its vendor on the CNCF blog. It grows with the number of policies.
      • Timeout and failure policy: the Kubernetes admission webhook reference. Default 10 seconds, range 1 to 30. The default failure policy is Fail.
      • Safety net: rollback time is roughly failed checks × interval, per Flagger guidance. Recovery tiers come from DORA 2024 as summarised by secondary sources.
      • Checkpoint: published change policies at Stanford IT and BYU OIT ask for 2 to 10 business days of notice, with weekly CAB meetings. These are policy minimums, not measured waits. No measured aging data was found.
      • Provisioning: self-reported cases (three weeks to 12 minutes, 12.4 days to 2.1 hours), an IDC paper (vendor-hosted, dated) on automated private cloud, and an interview reporting about 16-week hardware lead times in 2021. These are the weakest numbers here.
      • Team size: State of Platform Engineering Report Vol. 4 (518 practitioners, vendor-sponsored). 2 to 6% of engineering headcount, 4.7% on average.
      • Environment: DORA links flexible infrastructure, usually cloud, to higher organisational performance and warns that lift-and-shift hurts. Control taxonomy: Google Cloud, Beyond guardrails.
